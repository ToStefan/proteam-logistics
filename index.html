WHO WE ARE

ProTeam Logistics, LLC is a full-service freight transportation company based in Burr Ridge, Illinois, serving shippers, manufacturers, and businesses across the continental United States. We specialize in dry van freight with a focus on safety, compliance, and on-time performance.

Our experienced team and well-maintained fleet allow us to provide consistent, dependable service — whether it's a single load or a dedicated route. We treat every shipment like our reputation depends on it, because it does.
